# AERIS — Phase 3, Part 4: Mission 03 — Mercury Contamination

## Mission integration
- Added a BEFORE card explaining how fish that eat other fish can accumulate more mercury and how mercury moves through food webs.
- Added two DURING cards: an evidence-based comparison of lower-, medium- and higher-risk fish examples; and the importance of asking where fish was caught because even lower-risk species may accumulate mercury near mining. The cards also explain that cooking does not eliminate mercury.
- Reworked the two existing field-trial questions so players apply the evidence: identify higher-risk species and choose a safe information-seeking action when fish may come from a mining area.
- Added an AFTER card with a practical action for this week: ask about catch location and consult current public-health guidance. It includes the supplied Ministry of Health advice for pregnant people, breastfeeding mothers and children regarding fish that eat other fish.
- The BEFORE and both DURING ideas must be verified before the field trial; the AFTER card is required before mission completion.
- Added a mission completion summary with verified ideas, first-try count and a concrete family/community action.
- Updated the mission notice in English and Spanish.

## Evidence sources used (from the user's supplied evidence bank)

- Ministerio de Salud de Colombia; Revista U.D.C.A. — advice for pregnant people, breastfeeding mothers and children.

## Accuracy notes
- The fish risk groups are presented as indicative examples from the supplied regional references, not as a guarantee of safety.
- The classification evidence includes La Mojana and Bajo Cauca context; the game explicitly says catch location matters and does not imply that a lower-risk label guarantees safety in Nechí or any other place.
- The game does not claim that appearance, smell or cooking can detect or remove mercury.
