## Accuracy safeguards
- The Tadó, Chocó method is not presented as proven local adoption in Bajo Cauca.
- Plant mucilage is described as a research alternative, not a guaranteed universal replacement.
- The 95% result is attributed to the cited study and is not generalized to every mine or operation.
- Potential livelihoods are not presented as immediate or automatic replacements for mining income.
