# AERIS — Phase 3, Part 3: Mission 02 — Illegal Mining

## Mission integration
- Added a BEFORE card explaining that gold-mining processes in Bajo Cauca can release mercury into water and air.
- Added two DURING cards: safe observation/reporting without approaching machinery or confronting anyone; and the Colombian prohibition on mercury use in mining effective July 16, 2018.
- Updated the mobile/desktop mission notice to identify the destination as a safe observation point and reinforce distance from machinery and unstable ground.
- The existing joystick traversal now represents moving to the marked observation point; existing field-trial decisions reinforce safe documentation and the effects of vegetation removal/excavation.
- The two DURING evidence ideas must be verified before the field trial begins. The AFTER card must be completed before the mission can be recorded as complete.
- Added a completion summary with verified ideas, first-try count and a safe action for the week.

## Evidence sources used (from the user's supplied evidence bank)
- Cordy et al. (2011) — Science of the Total Environment.
- Ley 1658 de 2013; prohibition effective July 16, 2018 (source supplied: El Heraldo).
- World Health Organization (WHO) — Mercury and health / routes of exposure.

## Safety and accuracy
- The game does not claim that every visible machine proves illegal activity. Players are instructed to observe only what is visible from a safe public place, leave, and report through a trusted adult or competent authority.
- Players are never asked to approach, sample, touch equipment/materials, investigate, or confront anyone.
- The mission teaches the mercury prohibition without claiming that all mining activity is illegal.
